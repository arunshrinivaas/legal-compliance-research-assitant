COMPANY POLICY: Remote Work and Expense Reimbursement

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Section 1: Remote Work
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days per week. The core working hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.

Section 2: Expense Reimbursement
The company will reimburse internet expenses up to $50 per month.
All reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

Section 3: Equipment
Employees will be provided with a company laptop. Keyboards and mice can be requested through the IT portal, up to a maximum value of $150 combined.
